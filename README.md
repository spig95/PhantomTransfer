# Phantom Transfer 💸

A prank payment simulator built with Flutter.

## About

Phantom Transfer is a harmless prank app that simulates a payment transaction flow:

* enter a name and amount
* start the "transaction"
* show a realistic-looking payment animation
* enjoy the reaction 😄

## How to Use

1. Open the app and enter the name and amount you want to display.
2. Tap **Continue** to open the payment simulation screen.
3. For the best effect, hold your phone close to a friend's phone, smartwatch, or payment card like you would with a contactless payment terminal.
4. Press the volume down button to trigger the fake transaction.
5. Watch the payment animation and confirmation screen.

## Support

* Android only.
* iOS is not currently supported.

## Disclaimer

Phantom Transfer is a joke/prank app for entertainment purposes only.
It does not communicate with payment systems, NFC, banks, cards, or digital wallets. No real payments are made.
